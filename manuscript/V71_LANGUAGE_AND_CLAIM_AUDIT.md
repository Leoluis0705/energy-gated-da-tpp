# v71 Language and Claim Audit

Date: 2026-08-09

## Scope

This audit covers the v71 main manuscript and Supplementary Information. It checks:

1. repeated interpretation of the same result;
2. claims that exceed the available evidence;
3. language that is so defensive that it unnecessarily weakens the contribution;
4. consistency between the approved abstract, the main text, and the SI.

No numerical result, figure source value, experimental protocol, or approved abstract wording was changed.

## Main revisions

- Renamed Section 7.4 from **Error Analysis and Evidence Limits** to **Evidence Scope and Limitations**. The new title matches its actual purpose and does not imply that the section is primarily a list of failures.
- Concentrated the essential evidence boundaries in Section 7.4 instead of repeating them in the Results, Discussion, and Conclusion.
- Replaced the vague phrase “robustness surface” with “parameter-response surface”.
- Clarified that a zero DFT-evaluability label records a failed or incompletely evidenced archived workflow under the stated protocol; it is not a claim that the material is intrinsically non-computable.
- Described the four completed LiCr2O4 calculations positively and accurately as candidate-level first-principles feasibility evidence. They are not presented as phase-stability or synthesis validation.
- Removed the alarming main-text phrase that the cross-scale relationship “remains unresolved”. The revised wording states the actionable fact: no validated compatibility mapping was used, so the proxy and DFT energies remain separate evidence layers.
- Shortened repeated warnings about the 20-record evaluator. Its sample size and acquisition-blind status remain explicit once where they matter.
- Strengthened the mechanism statement: archived sequence hashes and ablations attribute the observed reordering to the group-triggered correction branch, while the Mn-oxide control shows preservation of direct ranking when no effective replacement is available.
- Strengthened the conclusion without overstating significance: the archived replay and held-out cohort support an aggregate early-budget advantage, while the final recovery ceiling is preserved.

## Claim calibration

### Claims retained as supported

- Gate improves aggregate early-budget target ordering in the held-out cohort under the frozen protocol.
- The gain is concentrated at early budgets and does not increase the final recovery ceiling.
- Group-triggered correction is the mechanism responsible for the recorded sequence change in the archived ablation.
- The Mn-oxide control demonstrates fallback to the Greedy path when the selected grouping offers no effective replacement.
- Four generated LiCr2O4 candidates completed the reported relaxation and independent static workflows and retained distinct compact relaxed structures.

### Claims deliberately not made

- statistical significance from the held-out Wilcoxon test;
- independent prospective DFT-active-learning validation;
- thermodynamic phase stability, magnetic-ground-state identification, synthesizability, or application performance;
- equivalence of ALIGNN, MLIP, and PAW-PBE+U energy conventions;
- observed DFT success based on the post-selection evaluability model.

These boundaries are necessary safeguards, but they are now stated once and in proportion to the evidence.

## Redundancy and style checks

- Main-body prose decreased from 5,235 to 5,190 words.
- No em-dash chains or repeated template-style result summaries were detected.
- Exact searches found no remaining instances of the superseded phrases “demonstrated benefit”, “only 20 labelled”, “does not support an independent contribution”, “not a Gate-Greedy comparison set”, or the old Section 7.4 title.
- The approved Word abstract is byte-for-byte unchanged at the text level between v70 and v71.

## Build and visual QA

- Main manuscript: 17 pages.
- Supplementary Information: 11 pages.
- Both PDFs compiled twice without LaTeX errors, undefined references, undefined citations, or overfull boxes.
- The abstract page, revised Discussion/Limitations pages, conclusion, references, and final SI pages were visually inspected; no clipping, overlap, or missing text was found.

## Final assessment

The v71 wording is calibrated rather than defensive. It presents the supported algorithmic contribution clearly, retains the positive DFT feasibility evidence, and confines limitations to the specific claims that the current data cannot establish.
