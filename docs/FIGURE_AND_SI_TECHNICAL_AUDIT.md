# Figure and Supplementary Material technical audit

## Figure corrections

- Figure 3 panel titles were separated; panel b now uses the shorter title
  `DFT-evaluability audit`, preventing collision with panel c.
- The obscured `26 vs 19` annotation was removed from panel a because the same
  result is stated in the caption and main text.
- Source font sizes were increased for Figures 2, 3, 4, 7 and Supplementary
  Figure S1. At the LaTeX display widths, ordinary titles, axes, ticks, legends,
  and annotations are at least 8 pt. Smaller PDF text operands are limited to
  mathematical subscripts or superscripts.

  PNG/TIFF exports where generated by the retained scripts.

  statement.

  paths such as `SourceData/...`, `Structures/...`, and
  `Tables/generated/...`.

  record is `provenance/public_manifest_sha256.csv`.

  the Supplementary Material does not resolve inside the release package.
